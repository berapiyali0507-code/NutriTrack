🌿 NutriTrack – Daily Calorie & Nutrition Tracker

Make space for feeling your best.

📌 About the Project

NutriTrack is a daily calorie and macronutrient tracking website designed to help users understand their food habits and maintain a balanced lifestyle.
The website provides a personal space where users can track their daily food intake, monitor calories, understand macronutrients and keep an overview of their everyday wellness.
Our aim is to make nutrition tracking simple, accessible and beginner-friendly.

🎯 Project Objectives

- To help users track their daily calorie intake.
- To monitor macronutrients such as proteins, carbohydrates and fats.
- To provide an overview of daily nutrition.
- To encourage balanced food habits.
- To help users understand their nutrition goals.

✨ Features

- Dashboard: View your personal daily nutrition overview.
- Food Log: Record daily food consumption.
- Calorie Tracking: Monitor daily calorie intake.
- Macro Tracking: Keep track of proteins, carbohydrates and fats.
- Food Lookup: Explore food and nutrition information.
- Weekly Trends: Review nutrition patterns over time.
- Wellness: Keep track of everyday wellness habits.
- Profile & Goals: Set personal information and nutrition goals.
- Suggestions: Get helpful nutrition-related suggestions.

🛠️ Technologies Used

- HTML5 – For creating the structure of the website.
- CSS3 – For styling and designing the webpage.
- JavaScript – For adding interactive features and functionality.
